About ECOZIA

Growing fresh, pesticide-free fruits, veggies & herbs in smart greenhouses and delivering them weekly to your doorstep

Our Philosophy

At ECOZIA, we believe that fresh, organic produce should be accessible to everyone. We combine cutting-edge smart greenhouse technology with traditional farming wisdom to bring you the best of both worlds.

Smart-Farm Ecosystem

Our innovative approach uses smart greenhouse technology to ensure optimal growing conditions year-round. We partner with local farmers to create a sustainable ecosystem that benefits everyone. Our state-of-the-art facilities combine traditional farming wisdom with cutting-edge technology, ensuring that every vegetable, fruit, and herb is grown in the most natural and sustainable way possible.

Our Journey

ECOZIA was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to make fresh, organic produce accessible to everyone while supporting local agriculture and protecting our environment. Starting from a small greenhouse operation in Nuwara Eliya, we've grown into a trusted name in organic produce delivery. Today, we serve hundreds of families and businesses, bringing farm-fresh goodness directly to their doorsteps.

Our Commitment

We are committed to transparency, sustainability, and quality. Every step of our process, from seed selection to final delivery, is carefully monitored to ensure you receive the freshest, most nutritious produce possible. We believe in building long-term relationships with our customers, farmers, and the community we serve.
